Why arrests are not the same as prevention

Arrests and seizures measure police output, not whether crime was prevented or families feel safer.

Why under-reporting matters

Reported crime = actual crime × reporting probability. A fall could mean less crime, less reporting, or both.

What a proper evaluation looks like

Compare similar camera-covered and uncovered areas before and after installation, while accounting for population, policing intensity and reporting rates. Police credited several simultaneous measures for the decline; it cannot be attributed to S4 alone.
