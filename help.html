08 / GET HELP

YOU ARE NOT
ALONE.

This site cannot accept or act on crime reports. It is not an emergency service.

In an emergency

Move somewhere safe if possible and contact Pakistan Police at 15. For a medical or rescue emergency, call 1122. These numbers are for immediate assistance, not this website.

How to report a crime

Contact police at 15 or visit a police station to ask about filing a report (FIR). Keep any case reference and copies of documents. Do not share sensitive information through this student project.
