01 / THE ISSUE

THE ISSUE IS
NEVER JUST CRIME.

Understanding how crime and weak justice can reshape the finances and wellbeing of ordinary households.

What do we mean by law and criminality?

Laws set boundaries and protections. Police investigate reports, courts consider evidence, and fair consequences can help prevent future harm. When parts of this system fail, cases may be slow, investigations weak, crimes may go unregistered and people can lose trust in the process.

Why focus on the middle class?

People in poverty often face the harshest conditions; wealthy families may be able to pay for private security. Middle-class households can have something to lose but little financial cushion or protection. A robbery, vehicle theft or snatching may mean weeks or months of lost income, repairs, replacement costs and new spending on security.
