05 / HOW THIS CONNECTS

LIVED EXPERIENCE VS OFFICIAL FIGURES

Different evidence.
Different questions.

Registered street crime in Karachi fell from 71,105 in 2024 to 64,323 in 2025, according to Dawn. Yet in our survey most respondents had heard of robberies nearby, a majority reported having been a robbery victim, and fewer than one in three always felt safe walking alone at night.

These are different kinds of evidence: a small perception survey and registered police FIRs, which undercount crime. They cannot be directly compared. Dawn reporting ↗

06 / ABOUT THIS SURVEY

What this survey can and cannot tell us.

  • There were 54 respondents. Responses were collected via Google Form; respondent names and emails collected by the original form are not published or used anywhere on this site.
  • This was a self-selected sample and is not representative of all Karachi residents.
  • 31 of 54 respondents (—) hold a master's degree, so the sample is more highly educated than the wider middle class.
  • The “Where do you live” question was free text with varied answers, including a few areas outside Karachi; responses are not analysed by area.
  • Several questions ask about perceptions; “Maybe” answers are included as their own responses.
  • These results describe this survey only. They are not official statistics and cannot establish causes.
