WHY WE MADE
JUSTICE UNHEARD.

A Cambridge O Level Global Perspectives school project and awareness action.

Our aim

We chose Law and Criminality because it is relevant to our local perspective. We focus on middle-class households who may have something to lose but limited financial cushion or private protection. Our aim is to raise awareness of hidden financial and psychological costs, and how weak justice can worsen them.
