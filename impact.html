04 / THE HUMAN COST

THE COST DOESN'T
END AT THE SCENE.

Financial strain and emotional stress can continue long after a theft or robbery.

01 / FINANCIAL

One loss can ripple outward.

A snatched phone or cash may be replaced at once. A stolen motorbike or car can disrupt work and school. Burglary can mean replacing belongings and securing a home. Direct loss is only one part: repair and replacement, missed work, time reporting a case, borrowing and new security costs can all add up. A household without insurance or savings has little buffer.

02 / PSYCHOLOGICAL

When the mind stays on alert.

People commonly report stress, anxiety, constant alertness, changed daily routes and routines, and less trust in the justice system. These are possible responses, not a diagnosis. When justice does not follow, uncertainty can add to the burden.
